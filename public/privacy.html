Privacy notice

Last updated 19 November 2026.

Who we are

Fettle is run by James Boyce, trading as Fettle. He is the data controller. Contact: hello@getfettle.co.uk.

What we collect and why

  • Account details: your first name, email address and a securely stored password. We use these to run your account. Lawful basis: contract.
  • Your plan answers: your home, who lives there, what supplies you have, contacts you choose to add. We use these only to build your plan and book. Lawful basis: contract.
  • Health answers (optional): for example medicines, equipment that needs power, mobility, whether carers visit, or foods someone must avoid. This is special category data. Before you have an account, your answers stay on your device. We only save them to your account if you tick the box that says so, and only use them to tailor your plan. If you don't tick it, we save your plan without them. You can skip these questions, or untick Save health answers on your plan page at any time to remove them. Lawful basis: explicit consent (UK GDPR Article 9(2)(a)).
  • Family calls (optional): when you book a call, we keep your name, email, phone if you give it, who the call is for (first name, age band, first half of their postcode, whether they live alone or have carers), who might join, good times and any notes. To stop fake requests, booking runs a robot check (Cloudflare Turnstile) and we keep a scrambled, daily-changing code made from your internet connection for 2 days; it can't be turned back into the address. On the call, your Fettle coordinator records the plan: each home, who lives there, what would stop working in a power cut, who can help and how far away they live, and actions agreed. This can include health details (for example medicines, equipment that needs power, hearing or memory), which are special category data. We only record them when the person they're about agrees on the call, or someone with lasting power of attorney agrees for them, and we note that agreement. The plan is shared with the family through a private link: anyone with the link can see it, so only share it with people you trust. Messages you send through "Something's changed" go to your coordinator. Coordinators can also keep private notes that families don't see. Coordinators are trained, DBS-checked and only use what they need. When something is coming, such as a weather warning, your coordinator checks it against your plan using public sources only. We never research you or your family online. We keep the plan until you ask us to delete it or stop using the service, and then delete it within 30 days. Lawful basis: contract (with the person who books), and explicit consent for health details (UK GDPR Article 9(2)(a)).
  • Payments: handled by Stripe. We never see your card details. We keep a record of what you bought and when, for tax. Lawful basis: legal obligation.
  • Fettle Plus (optional subscription): we keep which plan you're on (Plus or Plus with Post, monthly or yearly), whether it's active, when it renews or ends, and Stripe's reference numbers for you and your subscription. For Plus with Post, Stripe collects your postal address at checkout and keeps it; we read it from Stripe only to post your pack, and don't store it in our own database. You can change or cancel your subscription, and update your address, on Stripe's billing page from your account. Lawful basis: contract.
  • Meals and refresh dates: the meals you pick are saved with your plan to make your menu and shopping list. When you tick something off, we save the date and work out when it needs checking again (for example, water after 12 months). If you've asked for reminders, we email you when those dates come round. Lawful basis: contract (your plan) and consent (the emails).
  • Weekly emails: only if you tick the box. Unsubscribe at any time. Lawful basis: consent.
  • Check-in reminders: emails when your 30-day and 90-day checks are due, then one a season, only if you tick the box. Every email has a one-click unsubscribe link. Lawful basis: consent.
  • Seasonal reminders without a plan: if you sign up at getfettle.co.uk/remind, we keep your email address, your first name if you give it, and the date you signed up. If you give the first half of your postcode, we use it to look up your council area and keep only the area name, not the postcode. If you tick "Help your council plan", your household is counted, with no name or contact details, in totals for that area, and only totals of 5 or more are shared. We also note which workbook page or link you came from. Lawful basis: consent. Every email has a one-click link to stop them.
  • Postcode: we only ask for the first part (for example OL11), to give you the right local contacts and rules. To find the council area, we send just that first part to postcodes.io, a free postcode lookup service. Your full postcode is never asked for or sent, unless you choose to join the emergency list (below).
  • Flood check: when you check whether your area floods, we send the centre point of your postcode area (from postcodes.io, not your address) to the Environment Agency's free flood data service, to find nearby flood areas and any warnings in force. Nothing that identifies you is sent.
  • Emergency list (optional): only if you choose "Join the list" and tick the box. We keep your address, full postcode and an emergency phone number in your account, beside your plan. During a declared emergency, and only once your council has signed an agreement with Fettle, we share a short summary with that council's emergency planners: who lives at your home, roughly how many days of water and food you have, and needs you've told us about, which can include health needs. They use it to decide who to help first. Nothing is shared before then, and you can leave the list at any time, which deletes these details. Lawful basis: your explicit consent.
  • Insurance policy check (optional): if you choose your policy PDF, it's read on your own device, by software (pdf.js) your browser downloads from cdnjs.cloudflare.com. The policy is never uploaded to Fettle or anyone else, and isn't kept after you leave the page.
  • Warning emails (optional): if you turn on "Email me warnings for my homes", we use the location of each home in your plan, and of family homes you've been invited to, to check official warnings every 15 minutes: Environment Agency flood warnings, Met Office weather warnings and UK Health Security Agency cold and heat health alerts. To work out which region a home is in, we send its approximate location (not your name or email) to postcodes.io. We keep a record of which warnings we've emailed you for 30 days, so you don't get the same one twice. Emails are sent by Resend. Turn them off with the link in any warning email, or untick the box. Lawful basis: consent.
  • Family homes, part of Fettle Plus (optional): if you link family homes, the family dashboard is built from the plans you can already see. Emergency mode keeps what's happening, who started it, which homes are affected, who's been marked as OK or needing help, and your notes afterwards, in the plan of each home affected, so everyone invited to those plans sees the same picture. Anyone you've invited to a plan can see that plan, including its check-ins. If you answer "Would you pay for this?", we count the answer anonymously. Nothing is charged. Lawful basis: legitimate interests, so your family can look after each other.
  • Fettle Communities (optional): if you join from a community's link (for example a parish council, church, mosque, club or local business), at the end of the check you choose whether to add your home to that community's anonymous totals. If you do, we store a yes or no for each essential (such as 3 days of water or a torch), and whether anyone at home is over 70, lives alone or is under 5. We don't store your name, address, postcode, account or plan with it. A random number kept on your device lets you update or remove your answers. Organisers only see totals: nothing until at least 5 homes have joined, and never a number of 1 or 2. Your name and how to contact you are shared only if you tick "Also give my name", and then only with that community's organisers. If you ask the community for help, your message, and any name or contact you choose to give, goes only to its organisers. "Stop sharing" deletes your answers and any help requests you sent. To stop made-up answers, joining runs a robot check (Cloudflare Turnstile), and we limit how many homes can join from one internet connection each day. For that we keep a scrambled code made from your connection's address, which changes every day, can't be turned back into the address and isn't stored with your answers. It's deleted after 2 days. If an organiser reports a burst of joins that look made up, we may remove the homes that joined in that time; if yours was one, it's added back the next time you open Fettle. Organisers give a first name, shown to other organisers, and the information they add (such as a meeting point and contacts) is shown to their members and printed in members' books. Lawful basis: consent.
  • Family circle (optional): if you make a plan for a relative, you can add family members' names, phone numbers, roughly how far away they live and what each will do, and the care agency's details. These stay in your plan to build the rota and the fridge sheet. We never contact these people. If you send the rota, it goes from your own phone. Lawful basis: legitimate interests, so your family can look after your relative.
  • Your progress: each time you do a check, we save a dated record of your answers so you can see how far you've come. Lawful basis: contract.
  • The anonymous count for your area, without an account (optional): only if you choose "Add my answers". We store your council area (for example Rochdale), which of the 14 preparedness measures your home has, which critical risks are open, your answers to the "Could you cope?" questions, and a few yes/no facts such as whether anyone is over 70 or there's a baby. We never store a name, email address, postcode or anything you typed. A random number, not linked to you, stops your home being counted twice. Nobody can look up a single home: only totals of 5 or more homes are ever shown. Because we can't tell which answers are yours, we can't delete them later, so only choose this if you're happy with that. Lawful basis: consent.
  • Money questions (optional): whether you could pay an unexpected £200 bill, whether you're behind on rent, council tax or energy, and whether you've used a food bank or skipped meals. You can choose "I'd rather not say". We use your answers to add the right help to your plan. They don't change your score. Lawful basis: contract.
  • How many people use the check: we count that a check was started or finished, with the date and, for finished checks, the council area. If you arrived from a link that names where it came from, such as a QR code in The 3-Day Household Emergency Workbook, a link from a council or community group, or a link someone shared with you, we count that too, and keep it with your plan if you save one. No cookies, and nothing that identifies you or your device. Lawful basis: legitimate interests (knowing whether the check works).
  • Anonymous totals for your council (optional): only if you tick "Help your council plan". We count your household in totals for your area, like how many homes have 3 days of water or could pay an unexpected bill. We never share your answers, health details or contact details, and never show a group smaller than 5 households. Lawful basis: consent.

We never sell your data. We don't use it for advertising. The shop and some product suggestions link to Amazon; Amazon sees only that you clicked. Links marked "paid link" earn Fettle a small commission if you buy; we never see what you bought.

Who processes it for us

  • Supabase: database and accounts, stored in the UK (London).
  • Cloudflare: hosting the website, and the robot check when a home joins a community or a family books a call.
  • Stripe: payments.
  • Resend: sends account emails, and weekly emails and check-in reminders if you opt in. Emails are sent from servers in Ireland.
  • postcodes.io: looks up the area for the first part of your postcode.
  • NHS Organisation Data Service: if you search for your GP surgery or pharmacy when making your book, we send your search words and the first part of your postcode to the NHS's public list of surgeries and pharmacies. Nothing that identifies you is sent.

How long we keep it

Until you delete your account. Purchase records are kept for 6 years for HMRC, without your plan answers.

Your rights

You can see, correct, download or delete your data. Delete your account any time from your home page, or email us. You can withdraw consent for health answers at any time by unticking Save health answers on your plan page. We then remove them from your saved plan. If you're unhappy, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

If someone else helps you

If a council, housing or charity worker helps you make your plan, they can see it, and your answers are only counted in anonymous area totals if you agree. Small numbers are always hidden.
