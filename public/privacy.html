Patch.How we use your data

How we use your data

Patch helps independent salons, barbers and beauty businesses see how they compare with businesses near them. This page says what we collect, why, who helps us run it, and what you can ask us to do.

Last updated 24 November 2026.

Who we are

Patch is run by the team behind Patch. Email us at hello@getfettle.co.uk about anything on this page.

What we collect

  • When you ask for a report or check your prices: your name, email, role, phone if you give it, which business is yours, and any prices you send.
  • When you use your Patch page: what you upload (takings exports, supplier invoices, price lists, renewal documents), the dates and figures we read from them, and the actions you mark as done. From takings files we keep daily and hourly totals, not single transactions.
  • About businesses in each area: public information such as names, addresses, ratings, published prices and opening hours, including what's listed on booking sites.
  • When you click a partner offer: that you clicked it, so we know which deals are useful.

We don't use advertising or tracking cookies. Your browser keeps a few settings on your own device so pages remember where you were.

Why we use it

  • To make your report and your Patch page, and to email you what you asked for (the Monday email can be turned off at any time).
  • To work out area figures. Your prices are only ever used without your name, combined with others, and never shown where fewer than three businesses could be told apart.
  • To keep Patch working and secure.

We rely on your request (to provide what you asked for) and our legitimate interest in running and improving Patch. Where we ask for your agreement, such as using your prices in area figures, you can withdraw it at any time.

Who helps us run Patch

  • Supabase stores our data, in London.
  • Cloudflare serves the website.
  • Resend sends our emails.
  • Anthropic (and, where used, OpenAI) read photos and documents you upload, and answer the questions we ask when checking how AI assistants describe your area. They don't use this to train their models under the terms we use them on.

Some of these companies may process data outside the UK, under the safeguards UK law requires.

If you click through to a partner, such as an insurer, an energy broker or a card payments company, they handle your data under their own privacy policy. We may be paid by them if you buy; it doesn't change what you pay.

How long we keep it

For as long as you use Patch, and up to 12 months after you last did, unless you ask us to delete it sooner. Area figures that no longer identify you may be kept.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, delete it, or stop using it. Email hello@getfettle.co.uk and we'll reply within a month. If you're unhappy with how we've handled your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).
