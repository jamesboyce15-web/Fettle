Who we are

What we collect and why

  • Account details: your first name, email address and a securely stored password. We use these to run your account. Lawful basis: contract.
  • Your plan answers: your home, who lives there, what supplies you have, contacts you choose to add. We use these only to build your plan and book. Lawful basis: contract.
  • Health answers (optional): for example medicines, equipment that needs power, or mobility. This is special category data. We only use it to tailor your plan, and only with your explicit consent, which you give by answering. You can skip these questions or delete them at any time. Lawful basis: explicit consent (UK GDPR Article 9(2)(a)).
  • Payments: handled by Stripe. We never see your card details. We keep a record of what you bought and when, for tax. Lawful basis: legal obligation.
  • Weekly emails: only if you tick the box. Unsubscribe at any time. Lawful basis: consent.
  • Check-in reminders: emails when your 30-day and 90-day checks are due, then one a season, only if you tick the box. Every email has a one-click unsubscribe link. Lawful basis: consent.
  • Postcode: we only ask for the first part (for example OL11), to give you the right local contacts and rules. To find the council area, we send just that first part to postcodes.io, a free postcode lookup service. Your full postcode is never asked for or sent.
  • Your progress: each time you do a check, we save a dated record of your answers so you can see how far you've come. Lawful basis: contract.
  • Anonymous totals for your council (optional): only if you tick "Help your council plan". We count your household in totals for your area, like how many homes have 3 days of water. We never share your answers, health details or contact details, and never show a group smaller than 5 households. Lawful basis: consent.

We never sell your data. We don't use it for advertising. Some product suggestions link to Amazon; Amazon sees only that you clicked.

Who processes it for us

  • Supabase: database and accounts, stored in the UK (London).
  • Cloudflare: hosting the website.
  • Stripe: payments.
  • [Email provider]: weekly emails and check-in reminders, if you opt in.
  • postcodes.io: looks up the area for the first part of your postcode.

How long we keep it

Until you delete your account. Purchase records are kept for 6 years for HMRC, without your plan answers.

Your rights

You can see, correct, download or delete your data. Delete your account any time from your home page, or email us. You can withdraw consent for health answers by removing them. If you're unhappy, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

If someone else helps you

If a council, housing or charity worker helps you make your plan, they can see it, and your answers are only counted in anonymous area totals if you agree. Small numbers are always hidden.
