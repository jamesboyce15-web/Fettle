Checking on an older relative or neighbour

5 minute read · Last checked 7 November 2026 ·

Older people living alone are often the most at risk in a power cut or cold spell, and the least likely to have a plan. You can make a big difference on your next visit.

Look round on your next visit

  • Water: is there bottled water in the house, about 9 litres for one person for 3 days?
  • Food: is there food that doesn't need cooking or a fridge?
  • Light: is there a working torch they can reach from the bed and the chair? Do they have spare batteries? Are they using candles?
  • Warmth: are there blankets, a hat and warm layers where they can find them?
  • Medicines: do they have a few days' supply? Does any need the fridge?
  • Phone: is their mobile charged? Does their landline or care alarm work in a power cut? Many don't.
  • Alarms: do the smoke alarms and carbon monoxide alarm work? Press the test button.

A 10-minute chat

Keep it calm and practical, not frightening. You might start with: "There's been a lot in the news about power cuts. If yours went off, what would you do?" Then agree:

  • who will check on them, and how (call, text or knock)
  • a neighbour who can look in if you can't get there
  • where a spare key is, or whether they have a key safe
  • what they'd do if they had to leave the house, and where they'd go

If the phones go down

In a long power cut, landlines and mobile signal can fail. Agree in advance: "If I can't reach you, I'll come round by" a certain time, or a neighbour will. Write it down for them.

Get them on the Priority Services Register

People of pension age can usually join their energy and water companies' Priority Services Register. It's free and you can do it for them with their permission. It takes about 10 minutes.

Make them a plan

Fettle's free check works for someone else too. Answer the questions about their home, and you'll get a plan and shopping list for their house. You can do it together on your next visit.

Get a plan for your home, free

Answer a few plain questions about your home and who lives there. We'll tell you exactly what you need, how much of it, and what to do first. About 3 minutes.
