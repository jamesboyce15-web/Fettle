Plain guides for when things go off.

Power cuts, water cuts, cold snaps. What to do, written for real homes: flats, families with babies, and people looking after someone. Free, no sign-up, and checked against official UK guidance.

New to this? Start with three things.

  1. Store 3 days of water: 3 litres per person per day.
  2. Keep 3 days of food that needs no cooking. Here are six meals.
  3. Join the Priority Services Register if anyone at home might need extra help.
