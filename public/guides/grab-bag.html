Packing a grab bag

4 minute read · Last checked 7 November 2026 ·

Only about 1 in 5 people have a bag ready to grab if they had to leave in a hurry. You don't need a special kit. A rucksack and things from around the house will do.

The list

  • Torch and spare batteries, or a wind-up torch
  • Phone charger and a charged power bank
  • Copies of ID, insurance documents and prescriptions, in a plastic wallet
  • Some cash, about £10 per person
  • Spare house and car keys
  • A few days of every medicine, and a copy of the prescription
  • Glasses, contact lenses and hearing aid batteries
  • A small first aid kit, hand sanitiser and wipes
  • Bottled water and long-life snacks
  • Warm layers, hats, gloves and a foil blanket
  • A notepad, a pen and a whistle
  • A list of important phone numbers, on paper

Add if you need them

  • Babies: nappies, wipes, ready-made formula and a change of clothes
  • Pets: food, a lead or carrier, and any pet medicines
  • Medical equipment: chargers, batteries and the supplier's 24-hour number
  • Children: a small toy or book

Where to keep it

By the front door or in a cupboard near it, where everyone at home knows it is. Tell them.

Check it every season: replace food and water before the dates, charge the power bank, and update medicines.

Where would you go?

Agree two places with your household: one nearby, like a neighbour's, and one further away, like a relative's in another town. Write down the addresses.
